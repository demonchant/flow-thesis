Options flow is evidence, not a conclusion. Flow Thesis Ledger tests a stated thesis against live Unusual Whales Flow Alerts.

I enter a ticker and describe the conditions I want to evaluate.

The app retrieves the live alert, normalizes and stores its fields, and shows the underlying reference price recorded with that alert. OpenAI proposes structured rules and states what remains uncertain.

The proposal is validated as a draft. It cannot place a trade, and the model does not decide the thesis outcome.

After reviewing the rules, I explicitly approve read-only monitoring.

A poll checks for new observations, avoids duplicate records, and evaluates the stored evidence with deterministic rules. The receipt makes the inputs and result traceable.

You can see what the available evidence supports, what it does not, and when it is incomplete. Flow Thesis Ledger is an evidence monitor—not a price prediction or trading bot.
